New in 1.13: studio lights. Place coloured spot lights around your molecule, each with its own shadow, shining through haze and floating dust.

## Mac App Store — What's New

Studio lights: light your molecule like a photo studio.

• Up to six spot lights around the molecule, each with its own place, colour, warmth, beam, brightness and shadow. Your colours, materials and settings stay exactly as they are.
• Seven presets to start from: three-point, softbox, spotlight, Rembrandt, neon, sunset and underlight.
• Lights mode (⌃L, or Tools ▸ Enter Lights Mode): drag a light's knob on the molecule, set its beam with the rings, aim it at the surface, and fine-tune every value in the inspector.
• Coloured shadows, one per light: a red light's shadow is where red doesn't reach. With ray tracing on, a light's Size softens its shadow.
• Haze and floating dust fill the beams, and shadowed lights cast light shafts through the haze.
• Bright coloured lights keep their colour, and Exposure tames a bright rig.
• Sessions and scenes save the lights, and scene movies blend from one lighting setup to the next.

Also in this release:
• SNFG glycan cartoons: each sugar drawn as its 3D SNFG symbol, plus a glycan topology view.
• Movie export at 1080p, 1440p or 4K, with quality presets and H.264, HEVC or ProRes.
• Use your own MSA server for structure prediction, picked from the Predict bar.
• Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts (⌘/) lists every shortcut, and Help ▸ RayMol Documentation opens the new documentation site.
• Save Structure lets you choose which objects to save.
• Fixed: glass on spheres and ball-and-stick, background gradients, labels over transparent surfaces, and several session-restore issues.

## iOS / iPadOS App Store — What's New

Studio lights: light your molecule like a photo studio.

• Up to six spot lights around the molecule, each with its own place, colour, warmth, beam, brightness and shadow. Your colours, materials and settings stay exactly as they are.
• Seven presets to start from: three-point, softbox, spotlight, Rembrandt, neon, sunset and underlight.
• Lights mode: drag a light's knob on the molecule, pinch it to move it closer, and long-press the molecule to place a highlight. On iPhone the light menu sits in the Lights bar with a pull-up editor; on iPad the orbit view and inspector float over the scene.
• Coloured shadows, one per light: a red light's shadow is where red doesn't reach.
• Haze and floating dust fill the beams, and shadowed lights cast light shafts through the haze.
• Sessions and scenes save the lights, and scene movies blend from one lighting setup to the next.

Also in this release:
• SNFG glycan cartoons: each sugar drawn as its 3D SNFG symbol, plus a glycan topology view.
• Movie export with quality presets, up to 4K on iPad.
• Use your own MSA server for structure prediction.
• Share Structure lets you choose which objects to share.
• Notes open full screen, and the iPhone camera button fades out while you work.
• After an interrupted session restore, RayMol offers to start empty.
• Fixed: glass on spheres and ball-and-stick, background gradients, labels over transparent surfaces, and smart punctuation in the console.
